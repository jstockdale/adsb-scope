Coherent second look (mode_s_coh.c): decode on the fixed test set (2026-11-18)

Method: experiments/eval_coh.py on the prep_2400.py test set (strong / mid / weak: 300 messages/s,
10 s each; the Madrid capture at two gains; 20 s of noise) plus prep_dense.py (busy: 1,000 messages/s,
dense: 2,000 messages/s, 5 s each). 2.4 Msps, 8-bit, streaming path.
Simulated sets: percent of extended squitters decoded / frames matching no truth frame.
Madrid: extended-squitter frames (no ground truth here; see coh_madrid_xcheck.txt). Noise: frames decoded.
In simulation every frame carries a new random address, so the known-address gate refuses every repair
there and the gated rows equal the default image; their Madrid columns show the real effect.
"recovered/s" counts every frame the re-slice decoded. About half of those on Madrid would have been
decoded from the next candidate position anyway; the frame counts show the net gain.
