Decoder cost in instructions per input sample (2026-11-10)

Method: valgrind --tool=callgrind on host builds (x86-64), total instructions
minus a start-up-only run, divided by the number of input samples. Inputs:
1 s of pure noise (sigma 7.8 LSB per rail) and a 1 s simulated scenario with
1481 messages, each resampled to the decoder's rate through the 8-bit model.

                                         noise    busy
ours, 2.0 Msps, -O2                         50      47
ours, 2.0 Msps, -Og (firmware setting)      54      50
dump1090-fa demodulator, 2.4 Msps, -O3      76      77

Where ours spends it (busy, -O2): IQ-to-magnitude conversion 49.6 %,
preamble scan / bit slicing (mode_s_detect) 44.8 %, frame decode and CRC 1.7 %.

Per second: ours 2.0 Msps x 48 = 96 M instructions; reference 2.4 Msps x 77 =
184 M (1.9x). These are x86 instruction counts, not ESP32-P4 cycles.

New 2.4 Msps demodulator (mode_s_2400.c), same method, -O2:
                                         noise    busy
first version (16-chip timing correlation on every gated sample)   114     120
final (sliding gate, joint gate, pulse-energy ranking,
       full preamble test on the best two offsets)                  70      77
Per second: 2.4 Msps x ~74 = 177 M instructions, 1.8x the 2.0 Msps demodulator.
