Method: experiments/replay_expect.py - the host build of the firmware decoder run the way the
device replays a file (2.4 Msps, streaming, DF18 not delivered, known-address cache aged by the
recording's clock). "messages" is what the first REPLAY: line reports; the other columns are on
the second REPLAY: line. Two files:

  dense.cu8      prep_dense.py: simulated, 2,000 messages/s, 5 s - the heaviest decode load.
                 Every simulated frame has a new random address, so the known-address gate
                 refuses every soft or 2-bit repair: the three `adsb dec` settings give the same.
  madrid_lo.cu8  the Madrid capture at the test set's low gain, 20 s - real traffic. Made by
                 `python3 decimate.py madrid.sigmf-meta madrid_lo.cu8 --rate 2.4 --scale 145.9`
                 (md5 d0e04586752c613e1ea7f48b826ecced).

Does float rounding move these counts? The coherent look is the only float code in the decoder.
Rebuilt twice with different rounding - once with fused multiply-add (-march=haswell
-ffp-contract=fast: 27 fused instructions) and once at -Og with contraction off - the host
decoder gives the same set of messages as the normal build on dense.cu8, madrid_lo.cu8 and
weak.cu8, with the look on, with and without the soft rescue. The P4's own atan2f/sinf/cosf
are not covered by that. So: the same counts are expected on the device; a frame or two of
difference with the look on would not be alarming; more would be a finding.
