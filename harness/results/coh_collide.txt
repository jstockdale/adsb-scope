Coherent second look (mode_s_coh.c): pairs of overlapping frames (2026-11-18)

Method: experiments/collide.py. Two DF17 frames per trial in complex baseband, each with its own
amplitude, carrier offset (normal, 100 kHz sigma) and phase; rendered at 12 Msps, box-averaged to
2.4 Msps, noise added, quantised to 8 bits, decoded by the host build. Idealised: rectangular
pulses, no multipath, exactly two frames per collision. It measures the decoder's logic, not a
channel. Three runs follow: (1) the second frame starts 0-100 us after the first, so at least
20 us of the two overlap; (2) it starts 100-120 us after, so only the tail of the first frame
is hit; (3) 400,000 collisions to count frames that were never sent.

==== (3) collide.py --stress 400000 ====

400000 pairs of overlapping DF17 frames, start offset 0,100 us, stronger frame 0-10 dB up (one gap per batch of 2000),
40 aircraft addresses per batch, each heard once cleanly first; seed 1
false = decoded frames matching nothing that was sent; real address = of those, frames carrying an aircraft's address

Reading (3)
- The coherent look adds 50,000-92,000 frames and no false ones (12 -> 12, 17 -> 18, 12 -> 12).
- The fix2 image stands apart: ten times the false frames of every other row, and 117 of its 124
  carry a real aircraft's address. By the repair that produced them: 113 exact 2-bit repairs
  (every one under a real address), 7 one-bit repairs, 1 soft rescue, and 3 DF16 frames accepted
  through the address/parity check. In a 2-bit case the frame's address field came through the
  collision, other bits did not, and one of the 6,216 two-bit patterns happened to match the CRC
  error (6,216 of 16.7 million syndromes: about 1 in 2,700 frames damaged beyond two bits). The
  known-address gate checks the address, which is right, so it lets them through.
- The other rows by the same count: default image 9 one-bit repairs and the 3 DF16; fix2 image
  with the coherent look 99 two-bit, 7 one-bit, 1 soft, 3 DF16; fix2 image without the 2-bit fix,
  with the coherent look, 7 one-bit, 2 soft, 3 DF16; soft rescue with the coherent look and no
  gate 8 one-bit, 7 soft, 3 DF16.
- The soft rescue tries at most 162 patterns, all confined to the least confident bits; the
  coherent look uses only that. Hence the last two rows: the fix2 image without its 2-bit fix
  has the false-frame count of the default image, and with the coherent look decodes as many
  frames as the fix2 image does with it (289,940 against 290,621).
- With the coherent look the fix2 image's count falls (124 -> 110) because a frame it recovers
  is jumped over, and the damaged candidates behind it never reach the 2-bit fix.
- "Per million decoded" is per frame decoded in a test where every frame is hit by another
  within 10 dB. On the air most frames are not, so the rate per decoded frame is lower by
  whatever share of frames collide; the ratios between rows are what carries over.
